# riprap Trademark Policy

"riprap" is the name of this project, and the name is not part of what the license gives away.

The [license](LICENSE) covers copyright and patents. Like almost every software license, it says
nothing about trademarks, which means it grants no rights in the name, the domain, or the project's
identity. This document says what those rights are, so nobody has to guess.

The purpose is narrow: a person who types `/plugin marketplace add influpert/riprap` should get the
thing this repository publishes. Everything below follows from that and nothing more.

## What you may do, without asking

- **Redistribute riprap unmodified** under its name, keeping the license and notices intact.
- **Say what your software works with.** "Compatible with riprap", "built on riprap", "a riprap
  configuration for Rails" are accurate factual statements and are fine.
- **Write about riprap.** Reviews, tutorials, comparisons, criticism, conference talks. You do not
  need permission to name a thing you are discussing, and this policy does not try to give you one.
- **Use the name in the required attribution.** The `Required Notice:` line the license obliges you
  to carry is not a trademark use you need separate permission for.

## What needs a different name

- **A modified version distributed to others.** Pick your own name. You may say it is "derived from
  riprap" or "a fork of riprap" — that is a factual statement about origin, and it is welcome. What
  you may not do is ship modified code *as* riprap, because then the name no longer tells anyone
  what they are getting.
- **Anything suggesting official status.** Do not use the name, or a name confusingly close to it,
  in a way that implies this project endorses, maintains, or vouches for your work. That includes
  marketplace entries, package names, and repository names positioned as the canonical riprap.
- **The domain and the identity.** `riprap.dev`, the marketplace entry, and any logo or wordmark
  belong to the project.

Note that a modified version distributed to others may also be restricted by the license itself —
see the Noncompete and Competition sections of [LICENSE](LICENSE). This policy and the license are
separate constraints, and both apply.

## If you are not sure

Ask before you ship, not after. Open an issue. The answer is usually yes, and it is much cheaper
for both of us than a rename after your users have learned your name.

This policy may change. Changes are not retroactive: a use that was permitted when you started is
not something we will come after you for later.
